In-play tennis win-probability model: research and proposal

Scope: ATP Challenger, ATP, and WTA best-of-three singles. Goal: P(T1 wins the match) at every point of the match, including the states where a short-priced favourite fades. Grounded in the Break Point repo (research/markov.py, predictor.py, live.py) and the published in-play literature. Prepared 14 Oct 2026.
