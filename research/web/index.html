When does the number actually change?

Challenger best-of-3 only. The set-1 winner still closes about 82% of the time — every year from 2023 through mid-2026. Comebacks are loud; they are not the tape. Paste a live price after set 1 and compare it to that base rate, not to the last two nights you watched.

    Challenger tape

    In this sample, Challenger rank-underdogs cash at about the same rate as ATP tour matches (~36%). Retirements run a bit hotter. When the rank gap is under 20, it is basically a coin flip — that is why a thin market jumps, not proof the old number was wrong.
